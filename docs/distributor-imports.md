# Distributor CSV settlement import (MOE-005)

This is a **manual exported-file normalization** contract, not a live TooLost connection. The provider's exact export schema must be verified against a real authorized export before supplying explicit column mappings. Imported rows are not automatically recognized revenue, paid royalties, or ledger disbursements.

Call `parseDistributorStatement(csv,{labelId,source,mapping})` with explicit header names for transaction identifier, date, ISRC, currency, gross, fees, net and payout status. A settled row also requires a settlement-reference column. Exact decimal strings become integer minor-unit strings without floating-point rounding, grouped by their original currencies.

The importer fails closed on duplicate transaction IDs, missing headers, invalid ISRC/date/amount, inconsistent gross/fee/net, and unsupported payment states. It produces stable per-label/source/transaction identities, provenance `imported_csv`, and `isLive:false`. Downstream catalog resolution, bank/invoice settlement reconciliation, transactional persistence, reversals, and signed authorized provider feeds remain required for full MOE-005 acceptance. Currency minor-unit exponent is two in this initial schema. The parser explicitly supports USD, EUR and GBP and rejects every other currency until its exponent and arithmetic are implemented and verified. ISO 4217 minor units are maintained by [SIX](https://www.six-group.com/en/products-services/financial-information/market-reference-data/data-standards.html); a three-letter shape alone does not establish the exponent.
